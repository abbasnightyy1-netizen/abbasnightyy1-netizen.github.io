Privacy Policy

Tokyo Community Bot · Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Tokyo Community Bot ("the Bot," "we," "us") collects when it operates on a Discord server, how it is used, and how it is stored. By adding or using the Bot, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data We Collect

The Bot only collects data necessary to provide its features. We do not collect data for advertising or sell any data to third parties.

CategoryExamples
IdentifiersDiscord user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, role IDs
Command usageWhich slash commands are run, when, and by whom, for functionality and rate-limiting
Moderation & logsModeration actions (warnings, mutes, bans), automod triggers, and server event logs (joins/leaves, edits, deletions) as configured by server staff
Tickets & applicationsTicket content and staff application responses submitted by users through the Bot's ticketing and application systems
Games & activityGame scores, participation, and related stats for the Bot's games features
Message contentOnly where required for a specific feature to function (e.g. automod scanning, ticket transcripts) — not stored or read outside that purpose

2. How We Use Data

  • To operate core features: moderation, logging, automod, ticketing, staff applications, and games.
  • To enforce cooldowns, permissions, and prevent abuse of commands.
  • To let server staff review moderation history and logged events for their own server.

3. Data Storage

Data is stored in a local database associated with the Bot's hosting infrastructure. Data is retained only as long as needed for the features above, or until a server removes the Bot / requests deletion, or a user's data is deleted per Section 5.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share collected data with third parties. Data collected on a given server (e.g. moderation logs, tickets) is visible only to that server's authorized staff through the Bot's own commands, and to us for the purpose of operating and maintaining the Bot.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, or deletion of, data associated with your Discord user ID by contacting us at abbas.discord12@gmail.com. Server administrators may request deletion of all data associated with their server upon removing the Bot.

6. Children's Privacy

The Bot is intended for use in accordance with Discord's own minimum age requirements. We do not knowingly collect data from users who do not meet Discord's eligibility requirements.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect stored data from unauthorized access, but no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Bot after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or data requests can be directed to abbas.discord12@gmail.com.
